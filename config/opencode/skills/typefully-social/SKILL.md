# Typefully Social

## When to Use This Skill
- Writing and scheduling threads for X (Twitter)
- Creating posts for LinkedIn
- Managing content across multiple platforms
- Tracking engagement analytics

## Workflow
1. Connect your social accounts in Typefully
2. Write content: use the editor for threads or single posts
3. Schedule: set publish times or use the queue
4. Preview how the post will look on each platform
5. Monitor analytics: impressions, engagement, follower growth
6. Repurpose: turn a thread into a LinkedIn post or blog draft
7. Analyze what works: review top-performing content regularly

## Rules
- Write for each platform's audience — don't cross-post verbatim
- Keep threads focused — one idea per tweet in the thread
- Schedule at optimal times for your audience timezone
- Engage with replies to boost visibility
- Review analytics weekly to refine content strategy
- Don't over-schedule — leave room for timely, spontaneous posts
