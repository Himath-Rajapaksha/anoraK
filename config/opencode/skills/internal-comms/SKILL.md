# Internal Comms

## When to Use This Skill
- Writing status reports that answer the right questions
- Creating engineering newsletters people actually read
- Building FAQs that reduce repeated support questions
- Communicating technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders

## Workflow
1. Identify the audience: what do they need to know and what level of detail
2. Structure the content: lead with the summary, details below
3. For status reports: what was done, what's next, what's blocked
4. For newsletters: highlight interesting work, not just status updates
5. For FAQs: collect real questions from the team, answer clearly
6. Review for clarity: would someone outside the team understand this
7. Publish and follow up on questions

## Rules
- Lead with the most important information — don't bury the lead
- Use plain language, not jargon — explain acronyms on first use
- Keep it scannable: headers, bullet points, short paragraphs
- Include links to detailed docs for those who want more
- Update FAQs when new questions emerge — keep them current
- Ask for feedback on communication format and frequency
